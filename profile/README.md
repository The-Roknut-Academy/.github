## 👋 About

**RokNut Academy** is a strategy and content hub for **Rise of Kingdoms**. We publish guides, ship free tools, and run community infrastructure to help players of every level make smarter decisions — whether you're a fresh governor in your first kingdom or a veteran planning your next migration.

Everything under this organization is open, practical, and built around one idea: **good tools and clear information make the game better for everyone.**

---

## 🛠️ What We Build

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 🌐 **RokNut Academy Website** | Our home base — guides, tools, and resources for Rise of Kingdoms players. |
| 🔢 **Kingdom Number Estimator** | Estimates upcoming kingdom numbers using seed-letter cycling, fed by live launch data from our Discord bot. |
| 🌍 **Multi-Language Translator** | A RokNut-themed translator with Discord emoji/markdown support and slang expansion, built for multilingual kingdoms. |
| 🧬 **RokNut Talents** | A commander talent-tree builder with current commander data. No analytics, no tracking. |

> 🔗 Browse the repositories in this organization for source, setup notes, and issues.

## 🤝 Contributing

Contributions, bug reports, and ideas are welcome.

1. **Find something to work on** — check the issues in the relevant repository.
2. **Fork and branch** — keep changes focused and well named.
3. **Open a pull request** — describe what changed and why.

A few ground rules:

- Keep the RokNut look and feel consistent in anything user-facing.
- Never commit secrets, tokens, `.env` files, or credentials.
- No analytics or tracking added to tools without discussion.
- Be respectful — this is a community project.

Not a developer? You can still help: report bugs, suggest tools, share guides, or join the Discord and tell us what you'd like to see next.
